My Biography

I'm a PhD student in the Department of Sociology at the University of Macau. I study criminological theory, cultural capital, and social stratification. My research focuses on social stratification and social mobility.

Beyond discussing whether people eventually succeed in changing their social status through mobility, my research examines the distance and gap between the structural "promise" and reality. My target groups include non-resident workers, rural residents who bypass local facilities and go directly to top-tier hospitals to seek medical help, and young people born in county towns who have entered the urban modern system.

I aspire to be a scholar who uses advanced international approaches to analyze Chinese society and cares about the current state of local welfare.
