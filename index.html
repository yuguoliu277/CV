Welcome!

你的项目名

I’m a PhD student in the Department of Sociology at the University of Macau. I study criminology theories, culture capital, and social stratification. My research focus on the social stratification and social mobility.

Beyond discussing on whether the people eventually successfully changed their social status through the mobility, my research examines the distance and the gap between the “promise” of the structure and the reality. My research target groups include non-resident workers, rural residents who skip the local level and go straight to top-tier hospitals to seek medical help and the young people who were born in county towns and entered the urban modern system.

I aspire to be a scholar who uses advanced international approaches to analyze Chinese society and concern about the current situation of local welfare.
