My Biography

I'm a Ph.D. student in the Department of Sociology at the University of Macau. I study stratification, cultural capital, criminology theories, and statistics. My research interests focus on social stratification, social mobility, health and addiction.

Beyond discussing on whether the people eventually successfully changed their social status through the mobility, my research examines the distance and the gap between the “promise” of the structure and the reality. The target groups of my research include non-resident workers, rural residents who bypass local hospitals and go directly to top-tier facilities for medical care and young people born in county towns who now live in the modern urban system.

I aspire to be a scholar who uses advanced international approaches to analyze Chinese society and concern about the current situation of local welfare.
